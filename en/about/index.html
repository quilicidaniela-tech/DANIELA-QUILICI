01 / Introduction

Daniela Quilici

Daniela Quilici is a French-Venezuelan visual artist who graduated from the École nationale supérieure des Beaux-Arts de Paris, where she studied from 2004 to 2009.

02 / Artistic Practice

Her practice encompasses drawing, painting and ceramics. Her work explores organic forms, movement and the transformations of matter, through an artistic inquiry in which gesture, colour and the properties of materials play an essential role.

04 / Artistic Journey

Artistic Journey

Her artistic journey has developed between Venezuela and France, through solo and group exhibitions, artist residencies and various studio experiences.

She lives and works in Cachan, in the Paris region.
