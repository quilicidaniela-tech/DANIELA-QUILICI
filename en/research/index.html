02 / Research

Matter · Transformation · The Living

Daniela Quilici's work develops through a direct engagement with materials, their properties and the transformations they make possible.

Matter

Matter as a starting point

On paper and canvas, diluted acrylic, pigments, ink, charcoal and pastel give rise to surfaces marked by traces, transparencies and overlapping layers. Forms gradually emerge through a dialogue between gesture, colour and the reactions of matter.

Ceramics

Evolution of the artistic inquiry

Ceramics has been part of Daniela's practice for several years and is now undergoing a particular evolution. While she previously favoured unglazed surfaces, she is increasingly exploring the possibilities of glazing and drawing on fired clay, particularly using ceramic pencils suitable for firing.

This approach allows her to rediscover in three-dimensional form a graphic freedom close to that of her drawings and paintings. The pieces take on ambiguous forms, somewhere between receptacle and sculpture, without being confined to a utilitarian function.

Perspectives

Perspectives

Drawing, painting and ceramics thus form part of a shared artistic inquiry, in which matter, colour and gesture move from one medium to another.
