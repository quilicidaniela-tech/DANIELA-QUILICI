03 / Introduction

Matter · Transformation · The Living

Daniela Quilici is a French-Venezuelan visual artist whose practice brings together drawing, painting and ceramics. Her work explores organic forms, movement and the transformation of matter, with particular attention to gesture, colour and the properties of materials.

A graduate of the École nationale supérieure des Beaux-Arts de Paris, she pursues an artistic inquiry in which drawing, painting and ceramics engage in dialogue. Ceramics, a part of her practice for several years, plays an important role in her current experimentation.
